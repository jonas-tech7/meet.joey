My Vision & Commitment as the Developer

These days, far too many projects follow the same pattern: Developers bundle their own coins up to a $20K–$40K market cap and then sell on their own community to secure a quick profit.

Most of them aren’t genuinely interested in building something. Their main focus is extracting as much money as possible before moving on to the next project.

I want to approach this differently.

My goal is to build something that people genuinely want to be part of. I want everyone involved to have the opportunity to benefit from the project’s growth. I want this to become one of the craziest runners to emerge from a bear market.

I’m not here to launch a coin, collect a quick profit and disappear.

I’ll be working on this project around the clock.

Where will my efforts go?

My personal goal is to push this project beyond the usual crypto audience.

I want to establish a presence across as many social media platforms as possible, especially TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and X.

I’ll be creating and posting videos consistently, experimenting with different formats, trends and styles of humor to reach completely different audiences.

The idea is simple: Make the project recognizable even to people who have never interacted with crypto before.

I don’t want our entire reach to depend on Crypto Twitter. I want the meme to spread across the internet.

How will the fees be reinvested into the project?

Have you ever received holder rewards, only to realize that the amount you received was practically worthless?

I don’t believe that distributing tiny amounts of fees to individual holders is an effective way to grow a project.

That’s why this project doesn’t rely on holder rewards or unnecessary mechanisms that take away from its actual growth potential.

Instead, available fees can be reinvested into marketing and visibility to benefit the entire community.

For example:

  • TikTok promotions to increase video reach.
  • Paid exposure to introduce the project to new audiences.
  • Content creation and marketing campaigns.
  • Experiments with different platforms to find what generates the most organic attention.

The idea is to turn the project’s available resources into something that can create value for everyone involved.

More visibility → More recognition → More people discovering the project → A stronger community.

Rather than distributing small rewards to a limited number of holders, I want to focus on building something that has the potential to grow much bigger.

Any reinvestment will be communicated transparently so the community can see where the resources are going.

$JOEY is a memecoin with no intrinsic value and no promise of returns. This is not financial advice. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
